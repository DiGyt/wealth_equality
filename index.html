🌍 Is your country really rich?

💡 Does a high average mean the typical person is rich? Not necessarily!

The average wealth of a country is all of its wealth added up and divided by the number of adults. It tells you how much money a person has on average. Imagine 10 people in a room: nine have $10 and one is a billionaire. The average says everyone in the room is rich.

But that is not what the typical person has. That is what the median shows: line everybody up from poorest to richest and look at the person standing exactly in the middle. In our room, that person has just $10.

Most countries look a bit like this room: many poor people, and a few extremely rich people. The average wealth metric can mislead on how wealthy the typical person actually is. The wider the gap between average and median, the lower a country’s wealth equality, and high inequality is often linked to lower trust and a stronger sense of injustice.
